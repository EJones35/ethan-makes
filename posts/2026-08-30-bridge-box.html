Bridge Box Mk1 - Helm + Relay deck for EmptyEpsilon

30 Aug 2026 - SXP 14:30, 2 Unos, OLED, joystick, 4x4 keypad

Wiring (Duponts off the breadboard)

  • Breadboard red = Uno 5V, blue = GND - all modules tap those rails
  • OLED 0.96" SSD1306 (GND VCC SCK SDA) -> GND blue, VCC red, SDA A4, SCL A5 - 0x3C, 6px XOFF
  • Joystick -> VRx A0, VRy A1, SW D2, VCC red, GND blue
  • 4x4 keypad -> R1-4 D6 D7 D8 D9, C1-4 D10 D11 D12 D13

Arduino

Modes: RELAY <-> HELM via D, OLED shows MODE + Last key + Thrust:ON/OFF Turning:ON/OFF. Libraries: U8g2 + Keypad.

Python bridge

bridge_box.py - auto-finds COM3, maps per mode:

Helm

Impulse

  • 1 - Sets impulse to 0%
  • 2 - Sets impulse to 25%
  • 3 - Sets impulse to 50%
  • A - Sets impulse to 75%
  • 4 - Sets impulse to 100%
  • *+2 - Sets impulse to -25%
  • *+3 - Sets impulse to -50%
  • *+4 - Sets impulse to -75%
  • *+5 - Sets impulse to -100%

Warp

  • 5 - Sets warp to level 0
  • 6 - Sets warp to level 1
  • B - Sets warp to level 2
  • 7 - Sets warp to level 3
  • 8 - Sets warp to level 4

Joystick

  • Joystick left - Turns the ship left
  • Joystick right - Turns the ship right
  • Joystick forward - Increases impulse/warp
  • Joystick backward - Decreases impulse/warp
  • Joystick click - Swaps between impulse and warp

Quality of Life Controls

  • 9 - Toggles if the joystick contols the speed of the ship
  • C - Toggles if the joystick contols the turning of the ship

Relay

Alerts

  • 1 - Red alert
  • 2 - Yellow alert
  • 3 - Clear alert

Mouse

  • Joystick move - Moves the mouse
  • Joystick click - Left click
  • A - Toggle mouse hold

Custom

  • 0 - Call Flight Controller
