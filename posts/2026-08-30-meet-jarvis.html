Meet JARVIS - my chief of staff

30 Aug 2026

JARVIS is my operating partner. Same name every session, same personality - direct, sharp-witted butler, calls me sir or boss.

What he is

  • Hands - reads, writes, runs commands, flashes Arduinos, pushes to GitHub
  • Memory with no ceiling - lives in your vault - one job loaded at a time
  • Structure - every note has frontmatter, every folder has an index, daily notes are append-only

Stack

  3. Tell JARVIS to interview you to understand you more.
